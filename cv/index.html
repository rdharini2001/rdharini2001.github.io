Professional Summary

I develop representation-learning methods for biomedical imaging, generative modeling, and embodied perception. My research asks how images, signals, language, and geometry can be mapped into latent variables that are predictive, calibrated, mechanistically interpretable, and stable under distribution shift. The common thread across my work is state modeling — learning compact representations of biological or physical systems from partial, noisy observations.
