abstract: "Sericulture (silk farming) loses substantial yield to silkworm disease, and disease sorting is still mostly manual. We build an autonomous sorting system that combines instance segmentation for individual silkworms with 6-DoF inverse-kinematics control for grasp planning and class-conditioned manipulation. The interesting engineering choice was not the segmentation model but the coupling between perception and control: condition the grasp policy on the predicted disease class so that healthy and diseased silkworms are placed differently, and propagate segmentation uncertainty into grasp confidence. Patent filed in India; method published in IEEE ICDCECE and Applied Research in Engineering Sciences."
