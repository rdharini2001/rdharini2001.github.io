abstract: "Auscultation-based cardiac screening is cheap and ubiquitous, but model deployment is bottlenecked by compute, not accuracy. We build a lightweight deep ensemble with attention mechanisms over phonocardiogram windows, designed to fit comfortably on edge hardware. The model is then embedded in an edge-to-cloud wearable system that also captures PPG, derives HRV, performs oscillometric blood-pressure estimation, and uploads to a telemedicine layer. The clinical claim is conservative — this is screening, not diagnosis — but the system-level claim is that a calibrated low-cost auscultation pipeline can be deployed in low-resource settings without giving up much accuracy."
