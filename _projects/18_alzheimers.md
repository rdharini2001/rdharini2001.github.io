abstract: "Alzheimer's disease produces correlated but non-redundant signals in structural MRI and FDG-PET — atrophy and hypometabolism that overlap in space but diverge in time. We compare classification pipelines that fuse MRI and PET at different representation levels (early voxel concatenation, mid-feature fusion, late prediction averaging) and find that mid-level fusion of region-wise features is the most stable on ADNI subgroups, while early voxel fusion overfits and late fusion underuses cross-modal interaction. The result is unsurprising, but the experiment was useful as a baseline against which more sophisticated multimodal foundation models can be measured."
