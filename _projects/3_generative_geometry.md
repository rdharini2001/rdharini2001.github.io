This work studies **generative modeling through the geometry of probability transport**. I analyze how learned velocity fields, interpolants, and transport paths reshape distributions; how curvature and density concentration affect likelihood calibration; and how representation asymmetries emerge along the generative path.

*Under review at NeurIPS 2026.*

### Questions
- How do curvature and density concentration along transport paths affect likelihood calibration?
- When and why do representation asymmetries emerge along the generative trajectory?
- What does the geometry of interpolants tell us about sample quality and mode coverage?
