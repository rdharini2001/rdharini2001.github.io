Work from the Indian Institute of Science on **embodied perception** for autonomous mobile robots:

- **Zero-shot embodied localization** combining foundation-model embeddings, keypoint geometry, and spatial consistency — improving RTAB-Map SLAM accuracy by >63% (ACM ICVGIP 2025).
- **Differentiable 3D scene representation** using anisotropic Gaussian primitives, SE(3) optimization, and differentiable rendering.
- **Uncertainty-aware multi-robot perception** with ensemble disagreement, MC dropout, and spatial uncertainty maps.
- **Neural compression for edge perception** on Qualcomm RB5 — >3× speedup, <2% accuracy degradation.
