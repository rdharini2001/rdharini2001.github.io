During my time at the Indian Institute of Science, I worked on **embodied perception** for autonomous mobile robots:

- **Zero-shot embodied localization** combining foundation-model visual embeddings, keypoint geometry, and spatial consistency constraints — improving RTAB-Map SLAM accuracy by >63% without task-specific fine-tuning (ACM ICVGIP 2025).
- **Differentiable 3D scene representation** using anisotropic Gaussian primitives, SE(3) optimization, and differentiable rendering for infrastructure-assisted localization.
- **Uncertainty-aware multi-robot perception** under occlusion and clutter, using ensemble disagreement, Monte Carlo dropout, and spatial uncertainty maps.
- **Neural compression for edge perception**: structured pruning, INT8 quantization, and distillation for real-time 6-DoF pose estimation on Qualcomm RB5 — >3× speedup with <2% accuracy loss.
