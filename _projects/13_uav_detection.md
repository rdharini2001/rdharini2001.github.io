abstract: "Miniaturized UAV detection is a small-object problem under aggressive conditions: low illumination, sensor distortion, clutter, motion blur, and a class boundary (drone vs. bird) that is genuinely hard. We build sparse ensemble networks that train multiple specialized detectors with structured weight-sharing, then prune aggressively at inference. The result is more than 5x inference speedup and more than 25 percent relative improvement in localization, with the additional finding that the drone-vs-bird boundary is most cleanly resolved by short-temporal-context features rather than single-frame appearance. Deployed in the IEEE ICIP 2023 Grand Challenge on infrared drone tracking."
