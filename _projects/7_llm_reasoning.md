Investigating whether large language models perform diagnostic reasoning as **structured inference**, or collapse toward **prevalence priors** under constrained clinical cases. The work reframes medical LLM evaluation around four operational primitives: constraint satisfaction, causal exclusion, evidence integration, and calibration under noisy observations.

*ICLR 2026 Workshop on Logical Reasoning of LLMs; Stanford AIMI Symposium.*
