abstract: "Near-infrared spectroscopy can in principle estimate blood glucose without a finger prick, but in practice the signal is dominated by inter-subject variability and skin optics. We build a NIR sensing front-end paired with a neural calibration model whose hyperparameters are tuned by particle-swarm optimization. The pipeline is validated on the Clarke Error Grid, the standard for clinical glucometer safety, and reaches Zone A/B performance on the test cohort. The result is more a feasibility statement than a clinical claim, but it suggests that the limiting factor in non-invasive NIR glucometry is calibration, not sensing physics."
