I develop statistical-learning and representation-learning frameworks that use **ocular structure as a non-invasive projection of systemic disease state**:

- **OCTA capillary remodeling** for coronary microvascular dysfunction (AJPC 2026, ACC 2026)
- **Retinal vascular and perivascular geometry** for cardio-renal-metabolic risk
- **Deep fundus representations** for pan-cancer risk and cardiovascular risk in cancer patients (ACC 2026)
- **3D OCT-derived morphology** for treatment-response modeling
