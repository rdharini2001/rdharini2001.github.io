abstract: "Volumetric OCT carries both spatial structure (retinal layer geometry) and spectral/intensity structure (tissue reflectance signatures), and most biomarker classifiers throw one of them away. We use a multi-branch model that processes spatial and spectral feature streams in parallel, fuses them at a late stage, and exposes gradient-based interpretability maps so that biomarker predictions are accompanied by spatial evidence. Macro F1 above 98.5 percent on the held-out cohort. The course-project framing kept the scope modest, but the multi-stream + late-fusion architecture has informed how I think about 3D OCT representation learning more broadly."
