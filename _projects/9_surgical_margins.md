A multitask deep learning framework that treats **surgical margin assessment** in breast cancer resections as structured prediction over tissue geometry. The model learns coupled **tumor-probability fields, margin-distance representations, morphology embeddings, and uncertainty maps** — turning intermediate states into *interpretable measurements* of residual disease risk.

*Collaboration with ARPA-H.*
