abstract: "Standard image-to-image translation networks happily hallucinate plausible-looking vessels that do not exist. For medical synthesis this is dangerous — the topology is the diagnostic content. We factorize unpaired cross-modal translation into low-frequency appearance transport (modality-specific intensity statistics) and high-frequency anatomical topology preservation (vessel geometry, connectivity, microvascular structure). The model is allowed to change what fundus imaging looks like, but is forbidden from changing what is actually there. Applied to fundus-to-OCTA synthesis, this reframes medical image generation as structure-preserving distribution alignment under weak supervision — closer to a physical inverse problem than to stylistic transfer."
