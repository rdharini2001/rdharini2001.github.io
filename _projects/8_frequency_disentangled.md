An unpaired cross-modal generation framework that factorizes image translation into **low-frequency appearance transport** and **high-frequency anatomical topology preservation**. The method constrains the generative map to preserve vessel geometry, connectivity, and microvascular structure while adapting modality-specific image statistics.

Applied to **fundus-to-OCTA** synthesis, this reframes medical image generation as **structure-preserving distribution alignment under weak supervision**.
