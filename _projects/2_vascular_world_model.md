abstract: "Most vascular imaging models treat morphology as a static feature bank. We treat vasculature as a generative state — a structured object whose branching topology, perfusion field, and treatment response are coupled state transitions, not pixel statistics. The vascular world model is conditioned on tissue context, disease state, and intervention, and is constrained to remain consistent with continuity-of-flow and bifurcation laws. The interesting question is not how realistic the synthesized vessels look, but whether the latent trajectory is faithful enough to support counterfactual reasoning: what does this microvasculature look like after a hypothetical therapy, and how does that displacement in latent space correlate with measured outcome?"
