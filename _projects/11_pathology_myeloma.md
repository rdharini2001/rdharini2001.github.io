**Plasma-cell and immune-cell organization** as a structured tissue system from bone marrow biopsies. The work uses deep learning and spatial tissue modeling to extract neighborhood geometry, local composition, immune–plasma interaction patterns, and architecture-level phenotypes associated with **sex-specific survival differences** in multiple myeloma.
