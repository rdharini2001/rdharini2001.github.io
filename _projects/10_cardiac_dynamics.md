Foundation-model methods for **cine cardiac MRI** that learn compact latent trajectories of cardiac motion, morphology, and remodeling from spatiotemporal sequences. I frame cardiac video as a **generative state-space problem**, combining representation learning, temporal attention, neural dynamics, geometry-aware embeddings, and counterfactual trajectory modeling.
