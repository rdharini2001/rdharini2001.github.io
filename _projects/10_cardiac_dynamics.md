abstract: "Cine cardiac MRI is a 4D observation of a low-dimensional physiological state — a beating heart whose motion, morphology, and remodeling lie on a much smaller manifold than the pixel space implies. We frame cardiac video as a generative state-space problem and learn compact latent trajectories from spatiotemporal sequences, combining representation learning, temporal attention, neural ODE dynamics, geometry-aware embeddings, and counterfactual rollouts. The clinical motivation: counterfactual trajectories ('what would this heart look like under therapy X?') are unavailable from supervised classification but natural in a generative state-space view."
