In the ViTAL Lab (Georgia Tech & Emory), I developed **multimodal sensing and interpretable machine-learning models for mild cognitive impairment** using wearable IMU and ambient sensing. The work formulates gait, balance, and behavior as noisy longitudinal observations of cognitive and functional state, with emphasis on clinically interpretable digital biomarkers and subgroup robustness.

*Podium presentation at AMIA 2025; under review at PLOS Digital Health.*

I also analyzed how demographic heterogeneity, feature distributions, and clinical covariates shape prediction error in longitudinal sensing models — testing whether digital biomarkers remain stable across heterogeneous patient groups.
